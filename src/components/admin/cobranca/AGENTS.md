# Cobrança
- Alvará receipts use cob_judicial_alvara_liberar and official settlements against original debts, with account/date, audit, retry protection and reversal-aware history; final discounts wait for all receipts and retract on any linked reversal. Existing Asaas receipt keys stay immutable after reversal; never replay a callback as new cash. Why: no duplicates or unearned discount.

- Agreement (acordo) Asaas charges are intents tagged acordo_simulacao_id anchored on the oldest open debt installment; their baixa (cob_acordo_baixa) spreads the payment oldest-first across the agreement's original installments, excess = juros, agreed discount only after the last part is paid. Why: no new receivable title, so no double counting.
- Judicial agreements (cob_judicial_acordos) are written only via cob_judicial_* RPCs and require the case forwarded to judicial. Plan items flagged asaas=true become one linked cob_simulacoes row (judicial_acordo_id, discount 0) so they reuse the official acordo Asaas flow, lote and baixa; alvará/court-deposit items never create charges. Cancelling the judicial agreement cancels that linked row. Judicial docs live privately under judicial/<party>/ in the comprovantes bucket. Why: mixed court deals (alvará + parcelas) without a second charging engine.
