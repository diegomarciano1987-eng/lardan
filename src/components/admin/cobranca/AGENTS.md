# Cobrança
- Alvará receipts use cob_judicial_alvara_liberar and official settlements against original debts, with account/date, audit, retry protection and reversal-aware history; final discounts wait for all receipts and retract on any linked reversal. Existing Asaas receipt keys stay immutable after reversal; never replay a callback as new cash. Why: no duplicates or unearned discount.

- Agreement (acordo) Asaas charges are intents tagged acordo_simulacao_id anchored on the oldest open debt installment; their baixa (cob_acordo_baixa) spreads the payment oldest-first across the agreement's original installments, excess = juros, agreed discount only after the last part is paid. Why: no new receivable title, so no double counting.
- Judicial agreements (cob_judicial_acordos) are written only via cob_judicial_* RPCs and require the case forwarded to judicial. Plan items flagged asaas=true become one linked cob_simulacoes row (judicial_acordo_id, discount 0) so they reuse the official acordo Asaas flow, lote and baixa; alvará/court-deposit items never create charges. Cancelling the judicial agreement cancels that linked row. Judicial docs live privately under judicial/<party>/ in the comprovantes bucket. Why: mixed court deals (alvará + parcelas) without a second charging engine.
- "Pago por outra forma" (asaas_pago_outra_forma) settles via the official engine with the user's own permissions, key asaas:outra_forma:<account>:<charge>, then cancels the Asaas charge (DELETE) and records the result in asaas_baixas_outra_forma; asaas_baixa_automatica refuses charges listed there. Unpaid agreements (no paid charge, first due + 5 days) are cancelled daily by sync-matinal via cob_acordo_vencido_encerrar, creating a cob_tarefa for the agreement author. Why: never charge or settle twice.
- Legal master spreadsheet (0229/0230): cob_judicial_processos holds many processes per person, keyed by CNJ number+CPF (else sheet ID+CPF); written only by cob_judicial_planilha_importar (preview for cob_pode, apply Master only, audited by lote). People matched only by unique CPF; unmatched rows are kept without party. Apply fills only empty cob_judicial fields and moves non-extinct matched people to the Judicial stage; never touches finance. Why: the lawyer's sheet is the source of legal data without guessing identities.
- Kanban (0231): columns are derived by colunaDoDevedor (src/lib/cobranca-kanban.ts) from the stored etapa + cob_acordos_situacao (active acordo, overdue Asaas parcel); no new etapa values. Régua Asaas and Acordo atrasado are not drop targets; legacy "promessa" shows under Em negociação. One prorrogação per case via cob_acordo_prorrogar (record only, never edits Asaas); after it, >5 days late offers a manual move to Judicial. KPIs by period come from cob_kpis_periodo. Why: no data migration of existing cases and no automatic legal moves.
