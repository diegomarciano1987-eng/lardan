# Portal do representante

- Representative portal reads only via rep_portal_* RPCs gated by rep_portal_pode (own party as representante, or master/diretoria/financeiro/cobranca mirroring via /representante?rep=<party>); why: Daniel sees exactly the same numbers, scoped server-side.
- Representative actions (Asaas charge via repCobrar + rep_asaas_preparar, partial charge of an open installment via rep_cobranca_parcial (≤ saldo; rep_cobranca_avulsa without installment is refused since 0160), cash via rep_dinheiro_receber, cheque, reactivation, CRM moves/new leads) are allowed to whoever passes rep_portal_pode for the carteira that owns the target (owner representante or mirroring back-office), audited with the real caller. Why: Daniel operates the representative's screen exactly as the representative does.
- Since 0160 the representative sees only his own carteira (rep_portal_busca_todas is mirror/back-office only). Contacts and promises go through rep_cob_registrar/rep_cob_promessa into the same cob_interacoes/cob_promessas the Central de Cobrança reads. Reactivation with open debt never happens directly: rep_consultora_reativar_pedir creates rep_reativacao_pedidos for the Master (rep_reativacao_decidir); cheque/charge paths call rep_reativar_checado. Why: no reactivation without paying or approval.
- Representative app (0160): cobrança list rep_cobrancas_lista (faixas 1_30/31_60/61_90/90_mais, ordem recentes|maior) with last contact/promise from cob_interacoes/cob_promessas; contacts/promises only via rep_cob_registrar/rep_cob_promessa (same history as the Central). Every new charge is tied to an open installment and ≤ its saldo minus open partials (rep_cobranca_parcial → rep_cobrancas_parciais; rep_cobranca_avulsa refuses). Cash: rep_dinheiro_receber settles via fin_settlement_create on the company's "Retido Representante" account (rep_conta_retido) as the cobrança actor, idempotent by chave, receipt in rep_recibos. Field settlement: rep_acerto_campo_devolucao (scanned returns → sold = sent − returned, never below app-paid) then pre_baixa_fechar; pre_baixa_pode also admits the carteira's representative; staff conference stays pre_baixa_conferir. Reactivation with debt only through Master approval (rep_reativacao_pedidos). Visits in rep_visitas (registered visits also land in cob_interacoes). Funnel columns with rep_crm_etapas.sistema follow the Lardan decision (rep_crm_coluna_sistema) and refuse manual moves. Duplicate phones: preview contatos_duplicados_previa → Master contatos_duplicados_aplicar('REMOVER REPETIDOS') → undo by lote. Why: the representative's screen must match the official finance and cobrança history.
