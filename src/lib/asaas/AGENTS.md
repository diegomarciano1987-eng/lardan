# Asaas

- Drain the Asaas webhook queue only through `drenarFilaAsaas` (fila.server.ts); the morning sync and the manual button both call it, matching charge by account + external id. It only picks events with `processed_at IS NULL` and stops at a time budget; processed events left `na_fila` await a human decision and are counted separately ("aguardando decisão"). Why: notices that arrive before the mirrored charge must not stay stuck, and re-running hundreds of already-processed events froze the button.
- Received Asaas charges are booked automatically only through `asaas_auto_receber_nucleo` (0285): pg_cron `asaas-automatico` runs `asaas_auto_rodar` every 10 min (queue + engine) and the hourly `/api/public/asaas/extrato-hora` route fetches the statement; every decision lives in `asaas_auto_decisoes` (one active per charge, reversible only via `asaas_auto_desfazer`), limits and the master switch live in `asaas_auto_config`, and a Mesa "novo" on a `pay_` line must go through `asaas_extrato_lancar` (`fin_mesa_novo_conciliar` refuses it). Why: one `pay_` = one booking, every automatic booking has a reason and an undo.
- Statement-side automation (0286) runs only through `asaas_auto_extrato_nucleo` (inside `asaas_auto_rodar`, switch `asaas_auto_config.extrato_ligado`): planilha titles of the "Não identificado" party move to the charge's person only with a 1:1 confirmed match of equal value and their line links to the existing settlement (no new money); PAYMENT_FEE is booked in 5.1.3 only when the receipt's settlement carried no fee; notification/dunning fees become one 5.1.1 title per closed month; TRANSFER lines are only flagged when exactly one existing transfer matches. Every action is logged in `asaas_auto_extrato` and undone via `asaas_auto_extrato_desfazer`; undone keys are never redone automatically. Why: fix the Asaas balance without double-counting money already in the ledger.
- Asaas classification and maleta advances (0293/0294): a consultant's Pix/boleto with an open maleta (operacao/transito/acerto or open pre-baixa) and nothing open is booked as `adiantamento_maleta` (3.1.1 in her name, row in `asaas_maleta_adiantamentos`) and consumed only by the acerto engines via `asaas_adiantamento_consumir`; an advance already consumed cannot be undone from the panel. Asaas TED from a court without a registered process → `titulo_judicial` (config judicial chart/cost center); with a registered process → exception. Extrato step 1b (`fatura_baixa`) links a line to an already imported settlement only with a 1:1 match of invoice number, value and direction; boleto fees use `boleto_chart_id`, dunning has its own monthly title (`negativacao`, `negativacao_chart_id`), other fees keep their charts; manual internal-transfer links live in `fin_extrato_transferencias`. Manual reprocessing (`asaas_auto_receber(_, true)`) may redo an undone charge only when no settlement of it is active (new `:rN:` key); the routine never redoes. Why: the money is tied to the consultant without double-counting it at the acerto, and every automatic booking stays unique and reversible.
- Statement linking after redo and card anticipation (0295): `fin_asaas_extrato_autoconciliar` takes its key from `fin_reconcile_chave_livre`, so a redone `:rN:` settlement links its PAYMENT_RECEIVED/FEE lines again (`:refeita:N`). Extrato step 1c (`antecipacao_par`) flags as a neutral pair a PAYMENT_RECEIVED line and the same-day RECEIVABLE_ANTICIPATION_DEBIT of equal value and invoice only when that anticipation's GROSS_CREDIT line is already reconciled and the `pay_` has no active settlement; no booking, undo returns both lines to pendente and the routine never redoes. Mesa suggestions for Asaas receipts take the party from the charge, never from the statement-history key (identical for every Asaas receipt). Plpgsql subquery aliases in the extrato routine must not reuse loop record names (`r`, `g`). Why: redone bookings must not leave lines pending, anticipated card money is counted once at the anticipation, and generic history must not suggest a wrong customer.
- Asaas × Lardan divergence (0297): `asaas_espelho_upsert` maps `deleted=true` to `DELETED`; trigger `asaas_divergencia_detectar` opens one `asaas_divergencia_alertas` row per charge/type when a charge linked to an open receivable installment is deleted (and no other live charge covers it) or changes value/due date; the morning sync re-reads open mirrored charges that the listing did not return (rotating batch, `conferirAbertasSumidas`) because the Asaas list hides deleted charges. Phantom installments are cancelled only by a person via `asaas_fantasma_cancelar` (reason, single-installment title, no allocations, no live charge, judicial customers refused unless the same invoice reference is already on another installment) and undone via `asaas_fantasma_desfazer`. `fin_mesa_novo_conciliar` frees the `extrato:<line>` key of a cancelled title with zero net allocations (suffix `:cancelado:<id>`). Why: no phantom debt, nothing deleted automatically, an undone Mesa launch must not lock the line.
- Lardan settlement mirrored in Asaas (0299): a minute cron (`/api/public/asaas/saldo-sync` → `sincronizarSaldos` in saldo.server.ts) runs `asaas_saldo_reservar`, which sweeps every installment whose open linked charges exceed its balance (cancelled title = balance 0) or that was already handled and got a reversal. Before any DELETE the charge is re-read in Asaas (paid there → exception `pago_no_asaas`, never deleted); balance 0 only cancels; partial balance ≥ R$ 5 cancels and creates exactly one new charge via the official intent executor (`asaas_cobranca_preparar_nucleo`, `_saldo=true`, key `saldo:<job>:<balance>:<n>`), due max(today SP, old due), same billing type. Charges of agreements (acordo intents) are never touched; installments inside an active agreement, judicial customers and several open charges on one installment become exceptions decided in the panel (`asaas_saldo_decidir`, reason required). Every step lives in `asaas_saldo_sync`; the old intent goes `criada → cancelada` only under `lardann.asaas_saldo` with the charge already DELETED, and our own cancellation does not raise the 0297 "excluída no Asaas" alert. Why: what is open in Lardan must be exactly what Asaas charges, without ever booking money or double charging.
