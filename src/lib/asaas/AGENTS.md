# Asaas

- Drain the Asaas webhook queue only through `drenarFilaAsaas` (fila.server.ts); the morning sync and the manual button both call it, matching charge by account + external id. It only picks events with `processed_at IS NULL` and stops at a time budget; processed events left `na_fila` await a human decision and are counted separately ("aguardando decisão"). Why: notices that arrive before the mirrored charge must not stay stuck, and re-running hundreds of already-processed events froze the button.
- Received Asaas charges are booked automatically only through `asaas_auto_receber_nucleo` (0285): pg_cron `asaas-automatico` runs `asaas_auto_rodar` every 10 min (queue + engine) and the hourly `/api/public/asaas/extrato-hora` route fetches the statement; every decision lives in `asaas_auto_decisoes` (one active per charge, reversible only via `asaas_auto_desfazer`), limits and the master switch live in `asaas_auto_config`, and a Mesa "novo" on a `pay_` line must go through `asaas_extrato_lancar` (`fin_mesa_novo_conciliar` refuses it). Why: one `pay_` = one booking, every automatic booking has a reason and an undo.
- Statement-side automation (0286) runs only through `asaas_auto_extrato_nucleo` (inside `asaas_auto_rodar`, switch `asaas_auto_config.extrato_ligado`): planilha titles of the "Não identificado" party move to the charge's person only with a 1:1 confirmed match of equal value and their line links to the existing settlement (no new money); PAYMENT_FEE is booked in 5.1.3 only when the receipt's settlement carried no fee; notification/dunning fees become one 5.1.1 title per closed month; TRANSFER lines are only flagged when exactly one existing transfer matches. Every action is logged in `asaas_auto_extrato` and undone via `asaas_auto_extrato_desfazer`; undone keys are never redone automatically. Why: fix the Asaas balance without double-counting money already in the ledger.
- Asaas classification and maleta advances (0293/0294): a consultant's Pix/boleto with an open maleta (operacao/transito/acerto or open pre-baixa) and nothing open is booked as `adiantamento_maleta` (3.1.1 in her name, row in `asaas_maleta_adiantamentos`) and consumed only by the acerto engines via `asaas_adiantamento_consumir`; an advance already consumed cannot be undone from the panel. Asaas TED from a court without a registered process → `titulo_judicial` (config judicial chart/cost center); with a registered process → exception. Extrato step 1b (`fatura_baixa`) links a line to an already imported settlement only with a 1:1 match of invoice number, value and direction; boleto fees use `boleto_chart_id`, dunning has its own monthly title (`negativacao`, `negativacao_chart_id`), other fees keep their charts; manual internal-transfer links live in `fin_extrato_transferencias`. Manual reprocessing (`asaas_auto_receber(_, true)`) may redo an undone charge only when no settlement of it is active (new `:rN:` key); the routine never redoes. Why: the money is tied to the consultant without double-counting it at the acerto, and every automatic booking stays unique and reversible.
