# Financeiro
- Consultant-app client charges (financial_titles.origem='consultora') stay out of fin_titles_list, fin_overview, fin_pagar_receber, fin_cashflow(_detail) until any allocation exists (fin_pedido_consultora_aguardando); pagar-receber shows only a count/value note. Why: unpaid street invoices are not Lardan receivables (0351).
- Pix by CPF in the Mesa (0368): fin_mesa_por_documento (read-only, finance.statement.view) takes the CPF/CNPJ at the end of a bank 'entrada' line (never pay_ Asaas lines), matches only a single non-merged party (party_unificacoes duplicates excluded; 2+ = varios_cadastros, no suggestion), and plans oldest-first allocations with fin_installment_saldo, partial on the last, skipping installments in an active acordo. The Mesa pre-fills the vínculo with that plan; settlement still happens only when a person confirms via fin_reconcile. Why: consultants pay by Pix to the bank and the debt must be settled, never auto-settled or matched by approximate value.
- Transfers between own accounts from the statement (0369): the Mesa "Transferência entre contas" mode calls fin_mesa_nova_transferencia (reconcile + statement.flag + bank.manage), which creates the official transfer (fin_transf_criar_interno, key mesa:transf:<line>, :refeita:N after a reversal) and links the line and, optionally, the opposite line of the other account (same value, opposite kind, ≤3 days). fin_transferencias_parear_nucleo (inside asaas_auto_rodar every 10 min, switch asaas_auto_config.transferencias_ligado, and the "Procurar agora" button) pairs automatically only when BOTH lines cite the company CNPJ/legal name (fin_texto_conta_propria) and the pair is unique both ways; a line whose transfer already exists is only linked (modo existente). Every action lives in fin_transferencias_extrato; undo (fin_transferencia_extrato_desfazer, reason) returns lines to pendente and reverses a transfer created there; lines in that log are never paired automatically again. Why: own-account transfers must never become payables/revenue, and equal value alone never links.
